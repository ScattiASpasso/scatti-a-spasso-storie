About me

Hi, I'm Stefano Adami.

"Scatti a Spasso" comes from the desire to collect the moments when I took the time to stop and look at what often rushes past us too quickly. To share them the "old-fashioned" way. Without social media, algorithms, and likes at any cost.

For me, photography is passion. It's walking quietly between nature and art, mountains and cities. It's standing still waiting for a shift in light or bending down over a tiny detail. I don't look for the perfect shot.

Between landscapes, wildlife, and macro, I try to bring home honest images tied to the moment and place where they were born, without too many frills. If a photo makes you want to lace up your shoes and step outside, it has already done its job.

Photos can be freely downloaded using the dedicated button and republished as long as the source is credited. Zero cookies, zero tracking, just photography.

My gear

Canon EOS 5D Mark III camera body
My full-frame professional camera to play it safe with no compromises. With a lens attached it has significant weight, so it comes along on more "important" and never-casual outings, as well as sports and theater events.
Canon EOS 50D camera body
A semi-pro camera that is more compact and lighter than its pro sibling, and it was my first digital camera. Now used only paired with the 5D. When the moment matters more than the weight of two cameras.
Sony RX10 IV camera
1" CMOS sensor, 24–600mm optical zoom (f/2.4–4.0) The most used one. Light and versatile all-rounder compromise. It lets me go from wide landscape shots up to 600mm while keeping a quality that has little to envy a professional camera.
Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra
The lifeline against the fleeting moment when I don't have a camera with me. Even with a quad camera featuring a 200 MP main sensor, 50 MP ultra-wide, 50 MP 5x telephoto, and 10 MP 3x telephoto, like any smartphone it doesn't compete with real cameras, yet it's enough not to regret leaving them behind.
Canon EF 24-70mm f/2.8L USM lens
Professional standard zoom f/2.8 ultrasonic The quality all-rounder par excellence between wide angle and medium telephoto. Bright, solid, and immediate. The most used overall.
Canon EF 70-200mm f/2.8L IS USM
Professional telephoto zoom, constant f/2.8, stabilized Heavy and noticeable. Brought along on planned missions! When I go hunting for shots and distance matters. It compresses planes and separates the subject with soft bokeh.
Canon EF 100mm f/2.8L Macro IS USM
Macro and medium telephoto lens f/2.8 stabilized When I want to dive into tiny details, as well as for portraits and close-up glimpses with a clean, accurate rendering.
